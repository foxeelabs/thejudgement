# thejudgement
A role game betwen bad and good people
In this game there will be two teams:
the good people and the bad people

One part are the demons and the other part are the humans

the demons are traitors and are hidden between the humans
the humans need to find the demons and eliminate them

each round there will be a vote to eliminate one person
the demons will try to eliminate the humans
the humans will try to eliminate the demons
the game ends when all the demons are eliminated or when all the humans are eliminated

the game can be played with 4 to 10plus players
